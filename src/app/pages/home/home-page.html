The key, in one breath

David Lynch wants balance: to show us darkness so we can recognise the light. Late-80s TV made murder consumable, so Twin Peaks gave us one girl whose death mattered, and an investigation that was never meant to end. When the network forced the answer, the show became the thing it was fighting. Twin Peaks, says Twin Perfect, is a self-aware TV show about the balance of light and darkness on TV — and the meta is literal.
